      How it works

      No AI black box. Just clear rules that anyone can read, understand and improve.

      1

      Paste

      You paste the SMS or WhatsApp message into the box. It never leaves your device.

      2

      Scan

      A list of detection rules (patterns) is checked against the text, such as urgency words, OTP requests and fake links.

      3

      Score

      Every rule has a point value. The points of all the red flags found are added up and turned into a 0–100% risk score.

      4

      Explain

      You get the risk level, the exact words highlighted, a simple reason for each flag, and what to do next.

        Common scam types in India

        Learn the pattern once and you'll spot it everywhere.

        🏦

        Fake KYC / bank alert

        “Your KYC has expired, update now or the account will be blocked.” The link leads to a copy of your bank's site that steals your login and OTP.

        🎁

        Lottery, prize & cashback

        “You have won ₹25 lakh in a lucky draw!” You're asked to pay a “tax” or “processing fee” to claim a prize that doesn't exist.

        💡

        Electricity bill threat

        “Power will be disconnected tonight because last month's bill is unpaid. Call this number.” The “officer” then makes you install a remote app.

        💼

        Fake job / work-from-home

        “Earn ₹5,000 daily by liking videos.” After a few small payouts, you're asked for a registration fee or a big “investment”, and then they vanish.

        📦

        Courier & customs

        “Your parcel is held, pay ₹25 to reschedule delivery.” Or a fake “customs/police” call claiming drugs were found in a parcel in your name.

        🖥️

        Remote-access apps

        Scammers ask you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or an unknown APK “to fix a problem”. This lets them see your screen, OTPs and banking apps.

        📲

        UPI collect & QR tricks

        “Scan this QR to receive money.” You never need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR to receive money. A PIN is only for sending.

        🏛️

        Government impersonation

        Messages pretending to be TRAI, Income Tax, RBI, Aadhaar/UIDAI or police, threatening SIM blocking, fines or arrest unless you pay or call.

        Report a scam (India)

        If you lost money, speed matters. Report within the first few hours, since it gives the best chance of the payment being frozen.
